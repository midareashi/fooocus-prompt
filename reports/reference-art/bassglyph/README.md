## Shared visual language

- One young adult Japanese woman, usually isolated, depressed-looking, and emotionally distant rather than posed like a fashion campaign.
- Ordinary, slightly bleak Japanese spaces: an empty train, station platform, airplane cabin, cramped bedroom, or concrete housing estate.
- Plain white T-shirt or camisole, loose black or gray trousers, wired silver headphones, and minimal styling.
- Late-1990s/early-2000s point-and-shoot feel: heavy 35 mm grain, mild halation, soft detail, dirty green/cyan casts, direct flash, and imperfect underexposure.
- Simple readable composition with one strong color idea and generous environmental space.
- Occasional PlayStation jewel-case language: black vertical sidebar, restrained white type, tiny faux catalog marks.
- The art itself stays photographic and understated. Branding and type are best added after generation because image models render them unreliably.

The prompt configs reproduce these broad ingredients without naming the channel or duplicating a specific cover.
